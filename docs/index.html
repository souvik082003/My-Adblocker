Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Universal AdBlocker capabilities and safety.

Does this extension block ads on JioHotstar during live cricket matches?

Yes. Universal AdBlocker specifically monitors Hotstar's video player containers and live commercial breaks, automatically advancing pre-rolls and mid-rolls without disrupting live streams.

How does Universal AdBlocker bypass YouTube's anti-adblock detection?

Instead of aggressively dropping network packets which alerts YouTube's anti-adblock detection, Universal AdBlocker sanitizes ad placements in memory and uses high-speed 16x video acceleration to fast-forward through ads seamlessly.

Does this extension collect or sell my browsing history?

No. Universal AdBlocker has zero external network calls, zero tracking pixels, and zero telemetry. All processing is completely local to your browser.

Is Universal AdBlocker completely free?

Yes, 100% free and open-source under the Educational License. No subscriptions, no ads, and no premium upsells.
